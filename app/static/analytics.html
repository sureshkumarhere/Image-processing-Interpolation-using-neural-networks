Key Findings

Quantitative Architecture Takeaways

🛡️ Extreme Noise Robustness (σ=15)

OurNN maintains high structural fidelity under additive Gaussian noise, surpassing Bicubic by +6.50 dB on Set5 (30.13 dB vs 23.63 dB) and +4.48 dB on BSD100, proving its simultaneous denoising and super-resolution capability.

🚀 Parameter Efficiency

With only ~202k parameters, OurNN achieves competitive reconstruction fidelity with fraction-of-second CPU latency (~0.05s), positioned on the optimal Pareto efficiency frontier.

🔬 Ablation Verification

The ablation study confirms that residual skip connections (A1) and attention-gated feature refinement (A2) together improve SSIM and edge definition with less than 2% additional parameters over the plain backbone (A0).
